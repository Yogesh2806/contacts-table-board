Contacts: one dataset, two ways to look at it

A small team keeps business contacts in a Notion-style database. They need a table to scan fields and a board to see the pipeline — and they need to fix a contact's stage or notes from either view without losing their place.

Lead Prospect Qualified Customer
A

The job to be done

Three teammates (Arjun, Priya, Sara) own about ten contacts. Three moments matter:

Scan. “When I review my list, I want to see every field at a glance so I can spot gaps — missing emails, no owner.”
Move. “When a deal moves forward, I want to change its stage from wherever I am so the pipeline stays true.”
Return. “When I'm done editing, I want to land exactly where I was — same view, same scroll, same row.”
B

What must work

  • ✓One shared dataset of 8–12 fictional contacts
  • ✓A Table / Board switch over that same data
  • ✓Board groups: Lead, Prospect, Qualified, Customer
  • ✓Edit Stage and Notes from either view, with a clear Save and Cancel
  • ✓A saved stage moves the card and updates the table row
  • ✓No duplicate or stale records after any number of switches
  • ✓An empty board group and missing optional fields
  • ✓Closing the panel keeps your view; keyboard focus makes sense; works on a narrow desktop
!

Where it can break

Draft leaks. Typing in Notes must not change the table or board until you press Save.
Cancel lies. Cancel must restore the saved values exactly — even after five edits in a row.
Focus gets lost. After a stage save, the card has moved to another column. Focus has to follow it, not vanish.
The empty column. Qualified starts empty. It still needs a header, a count of 0 and a reason to exist.
Narrow screens. At 1024px, eight columns plus a side panel don't fit side by side.
Assumptions
Name and Stage are required; every other field can be empty. Stage is one choice of four. Only Stage and Notes are editable in this exercise.
Out of scope, on purpose
Backend, login, adding or deleting contacts, editing other fields. Search, filters and drag-and-drop are stretch goals, after the core flow is solid.
